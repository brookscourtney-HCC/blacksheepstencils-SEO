# Black Sheep Stencils — Autonomous SEO Engine

This repository powers a fully autonomous SEO and content system designed to grow organic traffic and increase stencil sales for BlackSheepStencils.com. The system generates keyword‑validated, citation‑checked, conversion‑optimized blog posts and publishes them automatically with no human editing or approval required.

## Goals

- Grow organic traffic from 250 to 1,000+ clicks per month within 90 days  
- Increase stencil sales by 30 percent  
- Build long‑term topical authority in beginner stenciling  
- Publish high‑quality content at scale with zero manual effort  
- Maintain consistent brand voice across all posts

## How the System Works

### 1. Keyword Selection  
The agent pulls live SEO data and filters keywords using strict criteria:  
- Keyword Difficulty under 30  
- Search Volume over 1,000  
Only high‑value, rankable keywords enter the content queue.

### 2. Topic Clustering  
Each accepted keyword becomes a cluster of 8 to 12 related posts, all internally linked.  
Clusters include:  
- Beginner guides  
- How‑to tutorials  
- Project ideas  
- Product‑adjacent content  
- Commercial intent posts  

This builds topical authority and improves rankings across the entire category.

### 3. Research and Brief Generation  
The agent analyzes the top 20 SERP results and creates a detailed content brief including:  
- Required sections  
- Questions to answer  
- Validated statistics  
- Verified citations  
- Internal linking targets  
- Product mentions  
- Conversion‑focused calls to action  

### 4. Drafting  
Posts are written using the Black Sheep Stencils brand voice profile.  
Rules include:  
- Answer first under every heading  
- Every number must have a validated citation  
- Include internal links to stencil collections  
- Include product mentions where relevant  
- Add calls to action to drive conversions  

### 5. Quality Gate  
Each draft is checked for:  
- Coverage versus brief  
- Structure and readability  
- Citation density  
- Internal linking  
- Product placement  
- Call‑to‑action placement  
- Voice consistency  

Failed drafts are automatically rewritten.

### 6. Publishing  
Approved posts are committed directly to the `/content/posts/` directory.  
GitHub Actions builds and deploys the site automatically.  
No human intervention is required.

### 7. Search Console Feedback Loop  
The agent reads Google Search Console data to:  
- Identify winning topics  
- Expand high‑performing clusters  
- Improve underperforming posts  
- Add new keyword opportunities  
- Optimize calls to action and internal links  

This creates a compounding growth engine.
